# Team 3.2 — Quick Trial Procedure

Summary of `BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md` for the day of the trials. The baseline runs on eEVA with OpenFace 3.0 and speech-to-text. An experimenter presses **Start trial** and annotates each trial from the trial report the module shows afterwards.

1. **Before class:** Complete the freeze (README, Freeze procedure) and note the version string, module, date, commit hash, analyzer, `auThreshold`, peak window, keyword list and fixed `strategy`. Open the Team 3.2 trial log tab (with the trial-report columns added). Print the 20 task cards (`TEST_SCENARIOS.md`), the key sheet (card to line ID) and the paper validity sheet. Look up the participant's order and lines in the table in `BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md`, section 5. Same room, laptop, webcam and headset for everyone; quiet room.
2. **Dry runs (finished):** The team ran a few trials and used **Capture AU** to check the AU values. They were recorded on the separate dry-run tab and never count. Capture AU is now commented out; **Start trial** is the only button.
3. **For each participant:** Assign the participant ID → ask consent (participation, and separately webcam and microphone processing) → read the instructions aloud (`BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md`, section 3), word for word.
4. **For each trial:**
   1. Write down the condition (pick it in the column E dropdown) and `Should fire?` (yes for A only).
   2. Hand over the card. The participant reads it and says "ready."
   3. Say: "Look at the camera and relax your face. When I press the button I will nod. At the nod, do what the card says." Check the face is relaxed.
   4. Press **Start trial**, nod once, and stay completely silent.
   5. Watch the participant's face and listen. The pose starts at the nod; the line starts within 3 s of the nod and is said once.
   6. As soon as the line ends, mark **valid** or **invalid** (and which part failed) on the paper sheet, **before** reading the trial report. Never use the AU4 value to judge this.
   7. Read the trial report on the panel: transcript, window length and how it ended (silence or time limit), sample size, AU values, AU4 peak against the threshold, facial cue, matched phrases, verbal cue, decision. Say "thank you," and let the participant relax their face for about 5 seconds.
5. **Log:** Trial ID, date and time, version, participant ID, condition, `Should fire?`, `Fired?` (the report's decision), and Notes (line ID, scenario order, exclusions with reasons, any mishearing). The outcome column is a formula. Add the facial cue (AU4 peak and threshold result) and the verbal cue (the scripted line's category), then the trial-report columns: transcript, matched phrases, cue flags, sample size, window length, how the window ended, threshold (`BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md`, section 6).
6. **Invalid performance:** If the pose or line is not performed properly, judged before reading the report, log it as its own row with `Should fire?` and `Fired?` blank, Notes "invalid pose/line, not counted, retried", and retry with the same card (at most 2 retries per card). A trial performed correctly always counts, even if the module misfires, fails to fire or fails to capture (including a misheard line); record what the module actually did. If the module crashes (no decision shown), log it and repeat once; how a crash is counted is still an open decision. Never delete a row.
7. **After:** Check that the conditions were tested equally. Read n overall, TP/FP/FN/TN, accuracy, precision, recall and F1 from "3.2 Check-in" K3:L11, and the per-condition and per-participant results from the "Summary" tab. Report setup, limitations and findings on the baseline slide (complete baseline due Mon Nov 9). State that the facial cue is AU4 alone and why AU7 was dropped (OpenFace 3.0 does not support it; PyFeat's AU7 readings were too noisy).
8. **End of session:** Save the log, thank the participant, give the one-line debrief (`BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md`, section 7).

**Reminders:** Dry runs do not count and are not recorded on the counted tab. The experimenter says nothing between pressing **Start trial** and the end of the trial. Do not change the keyword list, threshold, window, code or card wording during counted trials; any change means a new version and a restart.
